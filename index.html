Chapter 1: the pendulum

Maximum swing speed lives at the bottom of the arc. The scoring release is wherever the arc crosses the ball's line - the home page guide turns that one fact into every tip worth having.

Chapter 2: the modes

Ten solo puzzle levels build the release marker with free resets; the split-keyboard match (WASD and space left, arrows and L right) exams it under elbows and noise.

Chapter 3: goalkeeping

Nobody can run, so defending is arc parking: earlier, smaller releases that intercept rather than commit. Own goals certify that your timing read a line - the about page invites the complaints.
